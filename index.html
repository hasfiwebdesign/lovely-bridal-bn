(07)  FAQ

SoalanFrequently askedlazimquestions

01Bagaimana saya tempah temu janji?How do I book an appointment?

Isi borang di bawah atau WhatsApp kami terus. Lawatan ke butik adalah dengan temu janji sahaja.Fill in the form below or WhatsApp us directly. Boutique visits are by appointment only.

02Lebih baik beli atau sewa gaun?Should I buy or rent my gown?

Kedua-duanya boleh. Jahit tempahan menjadi milik anda; sewa tempahan ialah gaun baharu dijahit untuk anda dengan kos lebih rendah. Kami bantu anda pilih semasa konsultasi.Both are available. Made-to-own is yours to keep; made-to-rent is a brand-new gown sewn for you at a lower cost. We'll help you choose during your consultation.

03Adakah anda melayan pengantin bersaiz besar?Do you cater for plus-size brides?

Ya. Setiap gaun dijahit ikut ukuran badan anda sendiri, jadi potongannya dibuat khas untuk anda.Yes. Every gown is sewn to your own measurements, so the fit is made for you.

04Boleh tempah busana nikah saat akhir?Can you help with a last-minute nikah outfit?

Hubungi kami secepat mungkin dengan tarikh majlis anda. Kami pernah membantu pengantin dengan permintaan saat akhir.Contact us as early as you can with your wedding date. We have helped brides with last-minute requests before.

05Berapa lama gaun tempahan siap?How long does a custom gown take?

Bergantung pada reka bentuk. Kongsi tarikh majlis anda semasa temu janji dan kami akan rancang jadual cubaan.It depends on the design. Share your wedding date at your appointment and we'll plan your fitting schedule.
